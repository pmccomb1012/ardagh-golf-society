Society handicap rules are based on singles competitions only. Changes stack and are reviewed before being applied.

Current Society Rules
Win a singles day: -1.0 shot
Score over 37 points in singles: -1.0 shot
Finish last in a singles day: +0.5 shot
Score 24 points or less in singles: +0.3 shot
Rules stack. Example: win with 39 points = -2.0.
